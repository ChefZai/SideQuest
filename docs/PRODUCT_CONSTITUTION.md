# The SideQuest Product Constitution

Version 1.0

## Why SideQuest exists

Inspiration is constantly lost between “I found this” and “We actually did it.” SideQuest gives possibilities a safe, shared home so they can become experiences and, eventually, memories.

## Mission

**Transform inspiration into memories.**

SideQuest exists to help people build lives they will love looking back on.

## Core user problem

Ideas are scattered across messages, saved posts, and notes. Sharing can create pressure, planning can feel like work, and meaningful possibilities disappear. SideQuest removes that friction without turning life into another system to manage.

## Core product loop

Discover → Save → Share → Connect → Experience → Remember

Everything else is supporting infrastructure.

## Product principles

1. Capture should take seconds.
2. Sharing should feel safe, optional, and asynchronous.
3. Celebrate genuine shared excitement, not engagement metrics.
4. Reveal planning only when it becomes useful.
5. A completed Quest becomes a Memory; it does not become dead data.
6. When organization competes with emotion, choose emotion.

## Emotional principles

People should feel “I can’t wait,” “I want to share this,” “We actually did it,” and “I’m glad we saved this.”

They should never feel behind, guilty, pressured, or burdened by notifications.

## Design principles

Warm. Calm. Hopeful. Inviting. Thoughtful. Private. Human.

Never corporate, busy, gamified, transactional, or cold. Photography, whitespace, typography, accessibility, and emotional clarity matter.

## What SideQuest is

- A place to collect possibilities
- A place to quietly share them
- A place to discover mutual excitement
- A place to make them happen
- A place to remember them forever

## What SideQuest is not

SideQuest is not a task manager, workspace, calendar, habit tracker, social network, engagement feed, or productivity dashboard.

## Feature acceptance criteria

A feature must materially improve at least one part of the core loop. It should reduce friction, increase delight, strengthen relationships, or preserve emotional value. If removing it makes SideQuest meaningfully simpler without weakening the loop, remove or demote it.

## Success metrics

Optimize for first Quest saved, first Quest shared, first mutual reaction, first Quest made real, first reflection, and first Memory—not time in app, streaks, screen views, or notification volume.

North Star: **Meaningful Quests made real per active Space.**

## Long-term vision

After ten years, people should open SideQuest and see their lives: first apartments, friendships, trips, meals, dreams achieved, and dreams still unfolding. SideQuest should become the most beautiful private archive of a meaningful life.

## What we will not sacrifice

We will not sacrifice simplicity for features, manipulate people into returning, build addictive loops, pressure users with streaks, become a social network, or choose vanity metrics over meaningful experiences.
