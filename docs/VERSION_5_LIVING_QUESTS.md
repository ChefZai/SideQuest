# SideQuest Version 5 — Living Quests

Version 5 makes Quests feel like living possibilities instead of saved cards.

## Behavioral foundation

- A deterministic Hero Quest brings one meaningful possibility forward.
- Momentum describes real movement for experiences that do not have a natural percentage.
- Numeric progress appears only on Goal Quests with a valid current value and target.
- Milestones are highlighted Moments, not a separate task system.
- Home groups real activity into Shared Momentum, Continue Building, measurable Goals, a featured Memory, and relevant Life Chapters.
- Quest cards prioritize people, feeling, and movement over taxonomy.
- Quest Detail leads with emotional context, progress or Momentum, and one contextual next action.
- Journey timelines keep meaningful events prominent and preserve legacy Activity records.

## Compatibility

All behavior is additive. Existing Ideas continue to normalize as Experience Quests. Existing Goals without valid targets use Momentum. Existing Activities remain readable as Moments. No destructive migration or collection rename is required.

## Product rule

Never invent progress. Dates, trips, relationships, restaurants, and general experiences use human Momentum. Only naturally measurable Goals use percentages.


## Editorial experience

Version 5 leads with photography, human Momentum, curated Collections, optional Life Chapters, and reflective Memories. Power remains available through progressive disclosure while Home stays focused on the story unfolding now.
