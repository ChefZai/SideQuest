# SideQuest Version 5.5 — Editorial Product Polish

Version 5.5 is a craftsmanship release. It preserves the Living Quests architecture while making the product feel calmer, more cinematic, and more emotionally expressive.

## What changed

- Home now follows an intentional editorial rhythm led by photography.
- Continue Building appears before Shared Momentum.
- Quest, Goal, Memory, Collection, and Chapter cards share one visual family while retaining their identity.
- Quest Detail reveals the story before metadata and secondary controls.
- Collections read as curated albums; Life Chapters read as meaningful eras.
- Memories lean further into photography and reflection.
- Inspiration is now an editorial discovery surface instead of a rigid grid.
- Profile is framed as My Story rather than an account dashboard.
- Planner, Journey, Milestones, Quick Capture, dialogs, loading, and empty states share one motion and interaction language.

## Compatibility

No destructive migration is required. Firebase collections, permissions, authentication, templates, Momentum, Milestones, Goals, Planner data, Memories, and legacy records remain compatible.
