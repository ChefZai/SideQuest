## 1. Why SideQuest Exists

Life is filled with moments that begin long before they happen.

Someone sees a restaurant.

A friend sends a hiking trail.

A couple talks about Japan.

Someone dreams about learning guitar.

Most of those moments disappear.

They become buried in messages.

Lost in saved posts.

Forgotten in Notes.

Or never acted upon.

SideQuest exists because inspiration deserves a better home.

Not simply to be stored.

But to become reality.

## 2. Our Mission

**Transform inspiration into memories.**

Everything we build must strengthen that journey.

## 3. The Problem We Solve

People constantly discover experiences they'd love to have.

The problem isn't finding ideas.

The problem is everything afterward.

Ideas become scattered.

Friends forget.

Partners forget.

Nobody wants to pressure anyone.

Planning feels like work.

Eventually the idea disappears.

SideQuest removes friction between:

> "I found this."

and:

> "We actually did it."

## 4. Our Product Philosophy

SideQuest exists for one reason:

**Help people intentionally build lives worth remembering.**

We believe:

Life isn't improved through better organization.

Life is improved through meaningful experiences.

Technology should encourage living.

Not replace it.

## 5. What SideQuest Is

SideQuest is:

- A place to collect possibilities.
- A place to quietly share them.
- A place to discover mutual excitement.
- A place to make them happen.
- A place to remember them forever.

It is a bridge between inspiration and memory.

## 6. What SideQuest Is NOT

SideQuest is not:

- Todoist
- Notion
- Pinterest
- Google Calendar
- Instagram Saves
- Project management
- Habit tracking
- Social media
- Productivity software

If a feature makes SideQuest feel closer to those products, it must have a compelling reason to exist.

## 7. Our Core User

Our first users are people who naturally collect experiences.

Examples:

- Couples planning life together.
- Close friends.
- Families.
- Travel lovers.
- Food lovers.
- People with endless saved posts.
- People who constantly say, "We should do this someday."

## 8. The Core Loop

Every feature should strengthen one part of this loop:

```text
Discover
   ↓
Save
   ↓
Share
   ↓
Connect
   ↓
Experience
   ↓
Remember
```

Everything else is supporting infrastructure.

## 9. Product Principles

### Principle 1: Capture should be effortless

If saving something takes more than a few seconds, we've failed.

### Principle 2: Sharing should feel safe

No pressure.

No guilt.

No obligation.

### Principle 3: The product should celebrate mutual excitement

Not engagement metrics.

### Principle 4: Planning should appear only when needed

Never before.

### Principle 5: Memories matter as much as planning

A completed Quest is not finished.

It has become part of someone's life.

### Principle 6: Emotion beats organization

If organization comes at the cost of excitement, choose excitement.

## 10. Design Principles

Every screen should feel:

- Warm
- Calm
- Hopeful
- Inviting
- Thoughtful
- Private
- Human

Never:

- Corporate
- Busy
- Gamified
- Transactional
- Cold

Photography matters.

Whitespace matters.

Typography matters.

Emotion matters.

## 11. Emotional Principles

People should feel:

- "I can't wait."
- "I want to share this."
- "We actually did it."
- "I forgot about this."
- "I'm glad we saved this."

Never:

- "I have more work."
- "I'm behind."
- "I failed."
- "I have notifications to clear."

## 12. Feature Acceptance Test

Every new feature must answer **yes** to all of these.

Does it help someone:

- Capture inspiration?
- Share it?
- Discover shared excitement?
- Make it real?
- Remember it?

If not, don't build it.

Then ask:

- Does it reduce friction?
- Does it increase delight?
- Does it strengthen relationships?
- Does it create emotional value?
- Would removing it make SideQuest simpler?

If removing it would make SideQuest simpler, consider removing it.

Not replace it.

## 14. Long-Term Vision

One day, someone opens SideQuest after ten years.

They don't see data.

They see their life.

First apartment.

College.

Marriage.

Trips.

Restaurants.

Late-night adventures.

Weekend hikes.

Dreams they achieved.

Dreams they're still chasing.

SideQuest becomes the most beautiful archive of a meaningful life.

## 15. Success Metrics

Do not optimize for:

- Time in app
- Notifications sent
- Daily streaks
- Screen views

Optimize for:

- First Quest saved
- First Quest shared
- First mutual reaction
- First completed Quest
- First reflection
- First memory

Most importantly:

**Meaningful Quests made real.**

## 16. Things We Will Never Sacrifice

We will never sacrifice simplicity for features.

We will never manipulate users into opening the app.

We will never build addictive engagement loops.

We will never pressure users with streaks.

We will never become another social network.

We will never optimize for vanity metrics over meaningful experiences.

We will always choose products that help people spend more time living and less time managing.

## The North Star

Every company has a sentence that guides every decision.

Apple had:

> Technology should disappear.

Airbnb had:

> Belong anywhere.

For SideQuest:

> **Transform inspiration into memories.**

And the sentence every engineer, designer, marketer, investor, and future employee should memorize:

> **SideQuest exists to help people build lives they'll love looking back on.**

If five years from now every feature still supports that sentence, the product will stay focused even as it grows.
